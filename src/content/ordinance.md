## SECTION 1.
The above recitations are true and correct and are incorporated herein by this reference.

## SECTION 2.
Section 11.26.105, Downtown Worker Parking Pass, is hereby added to the Ashland Municipal Code as follows:

### A. Who may get a Pass.
The City shall issue a Downtown Worker Parking Pass without charge to a person employed at a place of business in the downtown parking area shown on the City’s downtown parking map. The person must show a pay stub dated within the previous 60 days that shows their name and employer, and give the address where they work.

### B. What a Pass allows.
A vehicle listed on a valid Pass may:

1. Park for up to twice the posted time limit in a space in that area with a posted limit of two hours or four hours, except in the plaza area described in AMC 11.08.080; and
2. Park in the City parking structure on Hargadine Street between 6:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. without paying a fee for that period.

All other parking regulations continue to apply.

### C. Limits.
A Pass lists one vehicle by license plate number, is valid for one year, and may not be transferred. A person may hold only one Pass at a time and may change the listed vehicle. The City may revoke a Pass obtained with false information or used for a vehicle it does not list.

### D. Administration.
The City Manager may adopt rules to carry out this section, including how a Pass is displayed and checked, and may limit the number of Passes. Within one year after Passes are first issued, the City Manager shall report to the City Council on the number issued and their cost.

## SECTION 3. Severability.
Each section of this ordinance, and any part thereof, is severable, and if any part of this ordinance is held invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, the remainder of this ordinance shall remain in full force and effect.

## SECTION 4. Codification.
Provisions of this Ordinance shall be incorporated in the City Code and the word “ordinance” may be changed to “code”, “article”, “section”, “chapter” or another word, and the sections of this Ordinance may be renumbered, or re-lettered, provided however that any Whereas clauses and boilerplate provisions (i.e. Sections 1 and 3–5) need not be codified and the City Recorder is authorized to correct any cross-references and any typographical errors.

## SECTION 5. Effective Date.
This ordinance takes effect on the ninetieth day after its adoption.
